Upgrade a vague answer

“Use better equipment” → “Use two light gates and an electronic timer to time the full track, removing variation from human reaction time.”

“Repeat for accuracy” → “Take at least three readings at every setting and calculate a mean to reduce the effect of random variation.”

“The gradient is resistance” → Check the axes: a graph of current against voltage has gradient 1/R. A graph of voltage against current has gradient R.

Graph workshop

First draw the graph on paper. Then reveal the points and model best-fit line to check your work.

Your task

  1. Label axes with quantities and units.
  2. Choose even scales that use most of the grid.
  3. Plot all five points accurately.
  4. Draw one straight line of best fit.
  5. Estimate current at 2.5 V.
  6. State what the gradient represents.

The grid shown is a model scale. For exam practice, choose and label your own scales on graph paper.

Read the graph and explain the gradient

At 2.5 V, current is approximately 0.20–0.21 A. Your estimate should come from your own best-fit line.

Gradient = ΔI / ΔV = 1/R, the reciprocal of resistance, also called conductance. Its unit is A/V (siemens).

A best-fit line follows the overall trend with points reasonably balanced on either side. It need not pass through every point or be forced through the origin.
